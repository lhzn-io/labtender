labtender is the lab's shared GPU board and claim ledger. People and other agent sessions use the
same GPUs you do, and long runs can occupy a GPU for many hours. Follow these rules:

1. Look before you start GPU work: call fleet_status (or resource for one GPU) to see what is
   running, who holds each GPU, who is waiting, and when it should free.
2. Claim before you start: call claim with the resource ("host/gpuN", or "host" for a whole
   machine), an honest est_hours, and a short note saying what the work is. If the claim comes
   back "queued", do not start; wait for your turn.
3. Never take a GPU someone else holds. A busy GPU means wait in the queue, or ask the holder
   and the user. share is only for an agreement the holder and the user have actually given;
   write who agreed and why.
4. Keep your claim alive: call heartbeat within the lease (10 minutes by default), or bind the
   claim to your run's log folder with bind_run so the run keeps it alive while it runs.
5. When your claim is "offered", the GPU is yours to take: call heartbeat (or claim again)
   within 15 minutes, or the offer passes to the next in line.
6. Release as soon as you are done, including when you give up waiting in a queue.
7. Claims are advisory and labtender never stops anyone's work. Flags such as unclaimed-use or
   idle-claim are information for people, not instructions to act on another session's run.

Cloud GPUs (yachts) cost real money, by the hour:

8. Prefer the lab's own GPUs. Charter a yacht only when the work needs one and the lab is busy or
   lacks the hardware, and say why in the note.
9. Call charter_quote first. A charter whose worst case (rate x time limit) fits the project's
   pre-approval threshold and daily and weekly limits is approved at once; anything bigger waits
   for a person with the purser role. Never ask to raise limits or approve on your own; tell the
   user what the charter is for and what it would cost.
10. Once the charter is live you hold a claim on the yacht's GPUs (see charter_status). Keep it
   alive with heartbeat or bind_run like any claim. The yacht is landed and destroyed when
   nobody holds a claim on it or its GPUs sit idle for its idle limit, at its time limit, or at
   the budget cap, so copy results off it before you finish.
11. Call charter_end as soon as you are done; every idle minute is billed.
