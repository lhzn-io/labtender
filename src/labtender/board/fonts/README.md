Josefin Sans and Martian Mono, latin subsets, from the Fontsource packages
(`@fontsource/josefin-sans` and `@fontsource/martian-mono`, 5.3.0). Both are under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1; the licence texts are `OFL-josefin-sans.txt` and
`OFL-martian-mono.txt`. They are bundled so the board works without reaching a font CDN.
